Privacy Policy, Key of Solomon Pentacle Finder

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The Key of Solomon Pentacle Finder is a self-contained reference application published by Enochian Alchemical Magick (www.enochian.org).

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. It works fully offline after the page loads. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Location

The app does not request or read your device location. If you enter a latitude and longitude for planetary hours, that entry is used only on your device to calculate the hours, and is not sent anywhere.

On-device processing

All calculations, including planetary hours, happen entirely on your device. Nothing leaves your device.

Questions about this policy: via www.enochian.org.
